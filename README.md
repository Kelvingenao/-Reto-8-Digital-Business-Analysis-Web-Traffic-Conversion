# 📊 Reto #8 – Digital Business Analysis: Web Traffic & Conversion

## 🏪 Business Context

An online store wants to evaluate the performance of its website to understand how visitors behave and which traffic sources lead to actual customer conversions. The goal is to optimize marketing efforts based on data-driven insights.

## 🎯 Challenge Objective

- Calculate total visits per traffic source (e.g., Organic, Paid Ads, Social Media, Email).
- Determine conversion rate (conversions ÷ visits) per traffic source.
- Identify the highest-performing channel.
- Visualize results using bar charts or funnel plots.
- Share a brief conclusion on which channels generate the most valuable traffic.

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview

Simulated dataset of 1,000 web sessions with the following columns:

- `SessionID`: Unique session identifier  
- `Date`: Visit date  
- `TrafficSource`: Organic, Paid Ads, Social Media, Email  
- `Device`: Desktop, Mobile, Tablet  
- `PagesViewed`: Number of pages visited  
- `Conversion`: 1 if purchase occurred, 0 otherwise  

## 📈 Key Findings

- **YouTube** leads in **organic content visibility**, making it ideal for long-term reach without paid investment.
- **Instagram** dominates in **influencer-driven traffic**, perfect for viral campaigns and brand awareness.
- **LinkedIn** shows the highest **conversion rate in SEO campaigns**, making it the most efficient channel for search-driven strategies.
- **TikTok** has lower visibility but performs well in **Ads conversion**, suggesting strong ROI for targeted paid efforts.
- All platforms are **well-aligned**, showing a healthy balance between visibility and conversion. No channel is underperforming—each has its strategic strength.

## 🧠 Strategic Recommendations

The best channel depends on the company’s goals:

- For **fast conversions**, invest in **Ads** on TikTok or Twitter.  
- For **brand reach**, focus on **Influencer campaigns** on Instagram.  
- For **SEO authority**, LinkedIn is the most efficient.  
- For **organic growth**, YouTube offers strong visibility without ad spend.  

Ultimately, decisions should align with business priorities—whether it's visibility, efficiency, or long-term brand building.
